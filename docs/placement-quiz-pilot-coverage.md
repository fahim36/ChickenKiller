# Week 1 placement coverage review

Reviewed by Codex on 12 October 2026 against the shipped v2026-10-10 learning objectives and existing sourced Question Banks. This records a content review, not a claim that a human Admin signed off. The learner release gate remains closed until the complete local pilot is verified.

Each Lesson has five Questions with a mandatory Question for each essential Concept. The four Stacks cover 17 Lessons: 25 Questions for Agentic AI Engineer and 20 for each other Stack. The per-Lesson rationale is stored alongside its blueprint. Existing immutable Questions and released Daily Challenges are preserved.

Pools exclude all frozen or scheduled Daily Challenge Questions. The existing banks suffice except Android Lessons 2 and 4, each requiring one additional variant. The new cache/set scenario tests primary-constructor equality; the receiver pair scenario tests static extension resolution. Their claims were checked directly against JetBrains [Data classes](https://kotlinlang.org/docs/data-classes.html) and [Extensions](https://kotlinlang.org/docs/extensions.html) on 12 October 2026. Sources and accessed dates are recorded on the Questions.

All essential Concepts have active MC/MS coverage. Auxiliary examples such as caching decorators are represented through their wrapping fundamentals, rather than expanding the Lesson blueprint to every named API. The .NET generics Lesson includes resource disposal, bringing it to five essential Concepts. These are self-assessment blueprints, not exhaustive certification exams.

Some pools have exactly five usable Questions. A never-exposed learner can place; an existing learner may have insufficient unseen coverage, and abandonment may exhaust eligibility. Selection must explain this and retain the normal Lesson path. Later Weeks have no placement plan in this pilot.
