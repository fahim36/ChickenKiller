# Passing placement completes Lessons without compulsory Retakes

Placement requires reviewed coverage of every Lesson and its essential Concepts, with at least 80% overall and within each Lesson. A passing Placement Quiz completes the Week's Lessons immediately and sends misses to optional Review: unlike Lesson Quiz completion, this accepts limited gaps in demonstrated breadth to provide a useful skip path. Existing pending Retakes in the target Week must be cleared before starting placement, and Milestones remain separately self-ticked.
