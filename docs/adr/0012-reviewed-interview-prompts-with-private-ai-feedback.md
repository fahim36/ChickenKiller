# Reviewed interview prompts with private AI feedback

Mock Interviews use reviewed Interview Prompts, rubrics and follow-up options rather than new written Questions. Extending ADR-0001's runtime-AI boundary, Gemini may produce private interview feedback and select a reviewed follow-up, but it never generates or modifies canonical Syllabus or Question Bank content. Keeping follow-ups reviewed grounds practice in approved teaching material and allows their selection within the bounded feedback request.
