# Reviewed launch interview coverage

Sixteen scenarios cover four distinct Concepts per Stack. The four initial seeds retain their permanent IDs; revision 2 adds an explanation-structure criterion. Historical sessions retain revision 1 snapshots. Twelve additional scenarios expand reasoning practice rather than adding written Quiz Questions.

Primary Sources were fetched on 11 October 2026; each prompt records its URL and access date. Editorial review checked the scenario claim, misconception and follow-up against these sources. All rubrics ask for correctness, reasoning, relevant limitations or trade-offs, and clear explanation. Neither accent nor a readiness percentage is assessed. Content counts demonstrate coverage, not measured learning effectiveness.
